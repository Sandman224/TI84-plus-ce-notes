Power transfer between AC systems

$$P_R+jQ_R=V_R\left(\frac{V_S\angle-\delta-V_R}{-jX}\right)$$
$$P_R=\frac{V_SV_R}{X}\sin\delta$$
$$P_{max}=\frac{V_SV_R}{X}\quad(\delta=90^{\circ})$$
$$Q_R=\frac{V_SV_R\cos\delta}{X}-\frac{V_R^2}{X}$$
If $P=0$:
$$Q_R=\frac{V_R}{X}\left(V_S-V_R\right)$$
P follows the angle $\delta$. Q follows the voltage difference $V_S-V_R$. Raise P by lowering X (series capacitor) or by raising $\delta$ (phase shifting transformer).
Three phase, per phase X and line to line voltages:
$$P_{3\phi}=\frac{V_{S,LL}V_{R,LL}}{X}\sin\delta$$
