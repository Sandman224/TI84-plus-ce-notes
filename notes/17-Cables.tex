Underground cables

Positive sequence shunt susceptance for single core and H type three core cables:
$$\omega C_{pos}=\frac{\omega\epsilon_r10^{-6}}{18\ln\left(R/r\right)}\;\text{mho/km}$$
Relative dielectric constant: oil filled 3.1, solid 3.6.
Cable cost is 3 to 20 times overhead. Larger C gives smaller $Z_c$ and higher SIL. Use manufacturer tables for series R and X.
