Surge impedance loading

Load equal to $Z_c$, with $\bar V_R=V_R\angle0^{\circ}$ and $\bar I_R=V_R/Z_c$:
$$\bar V_x=V_Re^{j\beta x}\qquad \bar I_x=I_Re^{j\beta x}$$
Flat voltage profile. Reactive power used equals reactive power produced:
$$\omega LI_x^2=V_x^2\omega C\qquad \frac{\bar V_x}{\bar I_x}=Z_c$$
$$SIL=\frac{V_{LL}^2}{Z_c}\quad(\text{three phase})$$
$$\begin{array}{c|cc}\text{kV}&Z_c\;(\Omega)&SIL\;(\text{MW})\\230&385&135\\345&275&430\\500&245&1020\\765&255&2300\end{array}$$
Loadability as a multiple of SIL:
$$\begin{array}{lll}\text{Length}&\text{Limit}&\times SIL\\<100\;\text{km}&\text{thermal}&>3\\100-300\;\text{km}&5\text{ percent V drop}&1.5-3\\>300\;\text{km}&\text{stability}&1-1.5\end{array}$$
For long lines the end to end angle $\delta\le40$ to $45^{\circ}$.
