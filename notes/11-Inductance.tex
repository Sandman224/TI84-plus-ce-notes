Line inductance

With $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$:
$$H_x=\frac{i_a}{2\pi x}\qquad B_x=\mu_0H_x=\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}\frac{i_a}{x}$$
$$\lambda_{a,i_a}=\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}i_a\ln\frac{\infty}{r}$$
$$\lambda_{a,i_b}=\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}i_b\ln\frac{\infty}{D}$$
$$\lambda_{a,i_c}=\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}i_c\ln\frac{\infty}{D}$$
$$\lambda_{a,total}=\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}i_a\ln\frac{D}{r}\qquad L_a=\frac{\lambda_{a,total}}{i_a}$$
$$L=\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}\ln\frac{D}{r^{\prime}}\;\text{H/m}\qquad r^{\prime}=0.7788\,r$$
$$L=2\times10^{-7}\ln\frac{GMD}{GMR}\;\text{H/m}$$
Transposed line, single conductor:
$$D=GMD=\sqrt[3]{D_1D_2D_3}\qquad GMR=0.7788\,r$$
Bundles, spacing d and conductor radius r:
$$GMR_2=0.8825\sqrt{rd}$$
$$GMR_3=0.92\sqrt[3]{rd^2}$$
$$GMR_4=1.02\sqrt[4]{rd^3}$$
More conductors raise GMR and lower L. Bundling also cuts corona. Best spacing is 8 to 10 conductor diameters.
