Three phase power

V is line to line voltage, I is line current, $\phi$ is the angle between phase voltage and phase current.
$$P_{3\phi}=3V_{ph}I_{ph}\cos\phi=\sqrt{3}\,VI\cos\phi$$
$$Q_{3\phi}=3V_{ph}I_{ph}\sin\phi=\sqrt{3}\,VI\sin\phi$$
$$\left|S\right|_{3\phi}=3V_{ph}I_{ph}=\sqrt{3}\,VI$$
$$PF=\frac{P_{3\phi}}{\left|S\right|_{3\phi}}=\cos\phi$$
Total power is 3 times the per phase power. The instantaneous total is constant:
$$p_{3\phi}(t)=p_a+p_b+p_c=\sqrt{3}\,VI\cos\phi$$
