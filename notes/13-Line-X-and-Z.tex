Line reactance and impedance

L and C are per phase (line to neutral). Analyze one phase at $V_{ph}=V_{LL}/\sqrt{3}$.
Series inductive reactance:
$$x_L=\omega L\;(\text{per length})\qquad X=\omega L\,l=2\pi fLl$$
$$x_L=2\pi f\cdot2\times10^{-7}\ln\frac{GMD}{GMR}\;\Omega/\text{m}$$
Shunt capacitive reactance and susceptance:
$$x_C=\frac{1}{\omega C}\qquad X_C=\frac{1}{\omega C\,l}$$
$$B=\omega C\qquad Y=j\omega C\,l$$
Series impedance:
$$z=R+j\omega L$$
$$Z=\left(R+j\omega L\right)l=Rl+jX$$
X here is the reactance in the power transfer equations.
