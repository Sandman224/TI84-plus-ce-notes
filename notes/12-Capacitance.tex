Line capacitance

$$D=\frac{q}{2\pi x}\qquad E=\frac{D}{\epsilon_0}=\frac{q}{2\pi x\epsilon_0}$$
$$v_{ab,q_a}=\frac{q_a}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\ln\frac{D}{r}$$
$$v_{ab}=\frac{1}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\left(q_a-q_b\right)\ln\frac{D}{r}$$
Line to neutral:
$$C=\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0}{\ln\left(D/r\right)}\;\text{F/m}$$
$$D=\sqrt[3]{D_1D_2D_3}\qquad \epsilon_0=8.854\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$$
