Phasors and impedance

$$v=\sqrt{2}\,V\cos(\omega t+\phi_v)\;\to\;\bar V=V\angle\phi_v$$
$$i=\sqrt{2}\,I\cos(\omega t+\phi_i)\;\to\;\bar I=I\angle\phi_i$$
$$X_L=\omega L\qquad X_C=\frac{1}{\omega C}$$
$$Z_L=jX_L\qquad Z_C=-jX_C$$
$$Z=R+jX\qquad X=X_L-X_C$$
$$\left|Z\right|=\sqrt{R^2+X^2}\qquad \phi=\arctan\frac{X}{R}$$
Series RLC in the time domain:
$$Ri+L\frac{di}{dt}+\frac{1}{C}\int i\,dt=\sqrt{2}\,V\cos\omega t$$
